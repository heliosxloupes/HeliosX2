## About

Surgical and dental loupes from HeliosX Vision help clinicians see fine detail while supporting a comfortable working posture.

We make professional magnification systems for surgeons, dentists, hygienists, residents, and clinical students. The collection includes lightweight Galilean loupes, conventional prismatic systems, and ergonomic prismatic options with transparent pricing and custom-fit guidance.

Our purpose is simple: premium clinical magnification should be accessible to the people who need it, without legacy markups or hidden pricing.

Explore the collection at https://heliosxvision.com or contact support@heliosxvision.com for product and fitting guidance.
