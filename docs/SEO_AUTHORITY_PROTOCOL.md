# HeliosX SEO Authority Protocol

Updated: 2026-09-13

## Purpose

Build durable organic visibility for HeliosX through useful clinical buying guidance, clear sourcing, honest commercial disclosure, and consistent brand entities across search and social platforms.

## 1. Google Preferred Sources

- Use Google's official publisher script and opt-in control.
- Label the action accurately: "Add as a preferred source on Google."
- Never describe HeliosX as Google-approved or promise placement in Top Stories, AI Overviews, AI Mode, or Discover.
- Publish fresh, original articles when there is real news, research, product data, or clinical guidance to share.
- Measure button visibility and opt-in clicks when Google's API exposes a reliable event.

Official reference: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/preferred-sources

## 2. Reddit-Intent Content

- Create a page only when it answers a real buyer question better than the current results.
- Reddit may appear naturally in the slug, title, and H1 when the page actually analyzes Reddit discussions.
- Link to the specific public threads summarized.
- Separate anecdotes from verified specifications, policies, and clinical evidence.
- State that HeliosX is the seller and is not affiliated with Reddit.
- Do not manufacture quotes, accounts, votes, consensus, or community participation.
- Do not create thin variants for every keyword or specialty.
- Review discussion pages at least every six months and update only when the substance changes.

## 3. LinkedIn Authority

- Use a LinkedIn Company Page named "HeliosX Vision."
- Preferred public URL: linkedin.com/company/heliosx-vision.
- Start the tagline and About section with "surgical and dental loupes" in natural language.
- Complete logo, cover, website, industry, company size, type, location, About, and specialties.
- Publish useful product education, fitting guidance, research interpretation, and company updates.
- Link relevant posts to the most useful page, not automatically to the homepage.
- Review LinkedIn Search Appearances monthly and adapt topics based on qualified searches.

## 4. Page Quality Gate

Every new authority page must:

- answer a specific buyer decision in plain language;
- contain original analysis, first-hand expertise, or clearly attributed sources;
- disclose HeliosX's commercial interest;
- use an accurate title, H1, canonical URL, date, author, and structured data;
- include useful internal links and at least one clear next step;
- avoid unsupported health, performance, pricing, or competitor claims;
- pass mobile review, production build, and live URL verification.

## 5. Measurement

Track:

- Google Search Console impressions, clicks, CTR, and average position by page and query;
- GA4 engaged sessions, product views, contact opens, add-to-cart, checkout, and purchase;
- AI referral traffic and cited-page landing sessions;
- Preferred Sources button interactions when measurable;
- LinkedIn followers, search appearances, website clicks, and assisted conversions.

Review weekly for technical problems and monthly for strategy. Judge content over 8-12 weeks unless indexing or policy errors require immediate action.

## 6. Release Checklist

- [ ] Search intent and user need confirmed
- [ ] Claims verified against current sources
- [ ] Commercial relationship disclosed
- [ ] Metadata, H1, canonical, schema, and dates checked
- [ ] Internal links and CTA added
- [ ] Mobile and desktop reviewed
- [ ] Build and type checks passed
- [ ] Sitemap and live URL verified
- [ ] Search Console indexing requested when appropriate
- [ ] Performance baseline recorded
